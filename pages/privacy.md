This policy explains how OTInexperiences handles personal information, cookies and third-party content.

**Last updated: 20 November 2026**

## Who is responsible?

OTInexperiences is a website dedicated to ornamental turning and related projects.

For privacy enquiries, please contact [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}).

## Information we collect

OTInexperiences is a static website built using Jekyll and hosted on GitHub Pages.

We do not operate user accounts, collect visitor registrations or intentionally use advertising or analytics trackers.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address and the contents of your message. We use it to respond to your enquiry and manage related correspondence.

The lawful basis for this processing is normally our legitimate interest in responding to correspondence, subject to your rights and the circumstances of your enquiry.

## Website hosting and server logs

Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub.

When you visit the website, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, browser information and request details to deliver the site, maintain security and operate its services.

Please see the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for further information.

## Cookies and similar technologies

We do not intentionally set first-party cookies for visitor tracking, advertising or analytics.

Some third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies when you choose to interact with their content.

### YouTube videos

Some pages contain videos hosted by YouTube, a service operated by Google.

To protect visitor privacy, embedded YouTube players are **not loaded automatically**. Instead, a placeholder is displayed explaining that the video is hosted by YouTube.

The player is loaded only when you choose to activate it by clicking the **Load video** button.

By activating a video, you allow your browser to connect to YouTube. Google may then collect information about your visit, including your IP address, browser information and interactions with the video, and may use cookies or similar technologies.

We use YouTube's standard embedded player (`youtube.com/embed/`). This player may use cookies or other technologies for purposes including video delivery, security, personalisation and analytics.

You are not required to activate embedded videos to browse the rest of OTInexperiences.

For more information about Google's processing of personal information, please see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Managing cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Most browsers allow you to block or remove cookies, although doing so may affect the operation of third-party services such as YouTube.

Further information about cookies and your rights is available from the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/).

## External websites and social media

OTInexperiences contains links to external websites, including GitHub, Instagram and Threads.

Following these links takes you to services operated by third parties, whose privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Ordinary external links do not automatically load content from those services into our website.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of external services before providing personal information.

## International data transfers

Third-party service providers, including GitHub and Google, may process information outside the United Kingdom.

Where UK data protection law applies, international transfers of personal information must be covered by an appropriate legal mechanism or safeguard.

Further information about these providers' data protection arrangements is available in their respective privacy policies.

## Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives you rights including:

- Access to personal information held about you.
- Correction of inaccurate information.
- Erasure of personal information.
- Restriction of processing.
- Objection to processing.
- Data portability, where applicable.

To exercise your rights concerning information we control, please contact us using the email address above.

You can also raise concerns with the [UK Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).
