This project allows you to simulate a Rose Engine to generate the cutting paths for your project.

<img alt="Rose Engine Project" class="alignnone size-large wp-image-59" height="304" src="/assets/placeholder-original-image.svg" title="Rose Engine Project" width="430"/>

The image is a view of a typical Rose Engine project showing the Toolpath pane and the Headstock pane. The image in the Toolpath pane is three dimensional and can be rotated etc to allow the path to be viewed from different directions. As you see in the image below when the image is rotated  we can see that the <a href=" http://blog.alanbattersby.co.uk/ornamental-turning-workbench/project-information/surface/" target="_self">surface</a> displayed in this instance is not flat and that the two paths lie beneath it.

<img alt="OTWB2" class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-64" height="300" src="/assets/placeholder-original-image.svg" title="OTWB2" width="400"/>

One of seven pre-selected viewpoints can be chosen. These are to look along the x,y or z axes in a positive or negative direction or to look at a slightly rotated view.  Alternatively the mouse can be dragged along the pane pressing the left button to pan up or down or pressing the right button to rotate. Finally the mousewheel can be moved to alter the scale allowing us to zoom into or out of the view.

The other pane shown is the <a href=" http://blog.alanbattersby.co.uk/ornamental-turning-workbench/rose-engine-project-2/headstock-display/" target="_self">headstock display</a>. This interface allows you to specify the various components of the headstock used to create the cutting path. Using this pane you can
<ul>
<li> Add / remove  Rosettes to / from the headstock spindle, alter their individual phase or edit their properties.</li>
<li>Specify the position of the rubber.</li>
<li>Specify the action of the spindle.</li>
<li>Assign the axes controlled by the spindle.</li>
<li>Specify a tool position.</li>
<li>Set up your chuck type.</li>
<li>Assign a squashing action if required.</li>
</ul>
Other panes associated with a  RoseEngine project are
<ul>
<li><a href=" http://blog.alanbattersby.co.uk/ornamental-turning-workbench/rose-engine-project-2/model-explorer/" target="_self">a path explorer</a> pane to allow you to add/remove paths from the project.</li>
<li>a <a href=" http://blog.alanbattersby.co.uk/ornamental-turning-workbench/rose-engine-project-2/path-generator-display/" target="_self">path generator</a> pane to allow you to specify angle ranges etc.</li>
<li>a <a href=" http://blog.alanbattersby.co.uk/ornamental-turning-workbench/rose-engine-project-2/code-generator-display/" target="_self">gcode generator</a> pane to view the output of the  gcode generator.</li>
</ul>
