I decided on trying to make a cutting frame that was modular in design. Also universal in that the angle the cutter approaches the work can be varied.Different heads can be added to the frame for use in different situautions. Too early to tell if this approach will be successful.
<ol>
<li>My modular cutting frame comprises of
<ol>
<li>A main column made from aluminium onto which various sub assemblies may be bolted.</li>
<li>The rear spindle that holds two an arrangement of two pulleys which may also be rotated around to any angle.</li>
