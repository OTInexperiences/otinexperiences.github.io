I have a split set-up, the headstock and the tool post are not connected.
<ul>
<li>The headstock has 3 axes of movement X, Z and rotation around Z which is the C axis. The Z axis is coincident with the headstock spindle.</li>
<li>The tool post also currently has three axis movement along U ,Y, W.  With Y being vertical,  W along the tools depth direction and X perpendicular to Y and W.</li>
</ul>
Often the set-up is so that the headstock X and the tool-post U axes are parallel as is the headstock Z and the tool-post W axes. However the headstock is sat upon a turntable so that the angle between the Z and W axes may be changed.

I mainly use a either a cutting frame or a drilling spindle both driven by a half hp  variable speed motor  via a poly belt from the sprung overhead .
