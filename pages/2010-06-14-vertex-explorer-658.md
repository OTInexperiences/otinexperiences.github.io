The Vertex explorer is a sub pane of the model explorer and allows you to view information about any vertex on path selected in the model view.

<a href="/assets/placeholder-original-image.svg"><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-660" height="343" src="/assets/placeholder-original-image.svg" title="vertex-explorer" width="439"/></a>

You can inspect the coordinates of a selected vertex in either cartesian, cylindrical or spherical coordinates depending on the option chosen. Any action points are marked by a red asterisk in the final column of the display.  Finally the selected point is highlighted in the path display.
