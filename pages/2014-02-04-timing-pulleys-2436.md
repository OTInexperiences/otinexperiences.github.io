I will require 3 pairs of timing pulleys, to connect in each case a stepper motor to a rotating spindle. The  ratio between the pulleys will be 2:1.
<h1>Spindle Pulley</h1>
This needs to have a 30mm bore plus a keyway 5mm wide in order to fit onto the pre-bought spindle.

A part was designed using the scad script kindly provided by droftarts and available <a href="http://www.thingiverse.com/thing:16627">here</a> on thingiverse.
