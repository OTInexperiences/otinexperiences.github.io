My Grose program limits the geometric chuck to 20 stages but that should be more than sufficient. The patterns starting B... are indexed using Thomas Bazleys values as first collected together by Bob Wade in his original DOS program. My program is just a more modern version of his original.

Where possible scale factors have been set to give a max radial value of 200mm
