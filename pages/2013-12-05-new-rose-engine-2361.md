I am building a new rose engine which may be either manually or computer controlled. I intend to do this by making the mechanisms that move the position of the headstock modular. So that either a manual or cnc method for control may be chosen according to the builders preference. The design will be illustrated by a series of images taken from a three dimensional model. At present I am using <a href="http://designspark.com/eng/page/mechanical">DesignSpark Mechanical</a> for the main drawings but also have to use SketchUp13.  See <a href="{{ '/assets/recovered/b17f234715-SOT-article.pdf' | relative_url }}">this</a> explanation of my ideas
