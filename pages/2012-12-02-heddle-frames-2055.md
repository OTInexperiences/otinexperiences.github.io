The frames holding the heddles are made from 20x20mm aluminium profile. In order to minimize the cost I decided to connect the frame together using m6 cap head bolts and not to use the special aluminium cube connectors. The ends of the frames are finished with small plastic covers. The top of each frame is to be connected to the shedding mechanism using texsolv cord looped through the two L shaped brackets.
