As my ambition is to control my rose engine using a dedicated processor, I am exploring the use of small micro processor boards. Because I wish to continue writing c# code I am looking at the Netduino and the Fez Panda 2 which are both roughly similar in functionality.

I envisage that the final system will have a small display for showing output status etc and a small keypad for entering data. It will also connect to my stepper motors and be able to download data to control the stepper motors.
