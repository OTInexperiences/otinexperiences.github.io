So I am basically following Dave Holly's design, with some changes of my own as I am able to make parts for myself. The first decision I made was not to use the schedule 40 pvc pipe he uses as this is quite expensive here in Britain. I opted to make my frame out of aluminium profile which I obtained from a company in Leicester called <a href=" http://www.aluminium-profile.co.uk/">KJN</a>.  This is not a cheap option, but I am extremely happy with the results.  The company cut the parts to the lengths I required and I also bought the correct connectors so that the frame was quickly bolted together in about an hour or so.It took this long because I had made a couple of adjustments to the design and had to re-cut a piece on the band-saw.
