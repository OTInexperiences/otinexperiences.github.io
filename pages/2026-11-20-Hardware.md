## Rose Engine Mark 2

The Rose Engine Mark 2 is a stepper-motor-driven rose engine designed and built as a standalone machine, separate from the conventional woodturning lathe.

The project includes the machine itself, its cutting equipment and the accessories required to hold and position workpieces.
