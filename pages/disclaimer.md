## Workshop and Machinery Safety

**WARNING: RISK OF SERIOUS OR FATAL INJURY**

Ornamental turning machinery, rotating components, cutting tools, electrical equipment and automated machine movements can present significant hazards.

Improper construction, modification, operation or maintenance may result in **serious injury, permanent disability, fatal injury or substantial damage to equipment and property**.

All projects, designs, techniques and operating procedures described on this website are provided for informational and educational purposes only. They should not be regarded as professional safety advice, approved operating procedures or certification that any equipment is safe to use.

Before attempting any project or procedure, visitors should:

1. Assess the risks associated with the machinery, tools and materials involved.
2. Ensure that appropriate safety guards, protective equipment and emergency stopping arrangements are in place.
3. Follow applicable manufacturers' instructions and recognised workshop safety practices.
4. Ensure that machinery and electrical equipment are properly maintained and operated by competent individuals.
5. Seek qualified professional advice where appropriate.
6. Never operate machinery or equipment without understanding its hazards, limitations and safe operating procedures.

Historical descriptions of machinery and workshop practices may not reflect current safety standards.
