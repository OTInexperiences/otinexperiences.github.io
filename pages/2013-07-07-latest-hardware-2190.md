<h2>Description</h2>
My rose engine is now split into two  parts  the headstock and the tool post.  And each is controlled by its own individual controller. The control electronics have been updated I am now using hardware stepper control using a FPGA based pci anything IO card, the 5i25 interface card made by <a href="http://www.mesanet.com/fpgacardinfo.html" target="_blank">Mesa Electronics </a> plugged into my PC. In turn this is connected to two <a href="http://www.mesanet.com/aiodaughter.html" target="_blank">7i78 daughter cards</a> each capable of connecting to 4 stepper motors. This gives me control for 8 axes with little load on the PC as all the stepper signals are generated by the FPGA on the 5i25.
<h3>The Headstock</h3>
This has movement along three axes.

<a href="{{ '/assets/recovered/9a85d0af24-headstock.png' | relative_url }}"><img alt="headstock" class="alignnone wp-image-2192" height="411" src="{{ '/assets/recovered/6fc3515849-headstock-956x1024.png' | relative_url }}" width="384"/></a>

The work rotates around the  Z axis and is able to move along the Z axis to give a pumping effect. The headstock can also move horizontally along the X axis, to approximately replicate the movement of a rocking headstock on a mechanical rose engine.
<h3>The tool post</h3>
This has movement along 4 axes although currently only three are used.
