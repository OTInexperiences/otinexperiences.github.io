Obviously you need to be able to sharpen your cutters in order to get a decent finish.  I use my Tormek water stone to grinding the initial shape with the help of a simple jig I made to fit the tormek system.

<figure class="wp-recovered-caption">
<a href="/assets/placeholder-original-image.svg"><img alt="" class="size-medium wp-image-932" height="177" src="/assets/placeholder-original-image.svg" title="IMG_0111" width="300"/></a>
<figcaption>Grinding jig for Tormek</figcaption>
</figure>

This allows me to set the angle of the face to be ground in two directions.

To finish my cutters I have constructed a small lapping wheel 150mm in diameter which is driven from the tormek using some poly belting.

<figure class="wp-recovered-caption">
<a href="/assets/placeholder-original-image.svg"><img alt="" class="size-medium wp-image-933" height="225" src="/assets/placeholder-original-image.svg" title="IMG_0108" width="300"/></a>
<figcaption>Lapping machine</figcaption>
</figure>

The base of the lapping machine is a perspex master disc onto which I can place other lapping discs which are held down by the screwed rim. I have a leather disc that holds the lapping compound supplied by tormek. But I also have three <a href="http://www.manchesterminerals.co.uk/acatalog/info_17_083.html" target="_blank">REZ- Discs</a> I bought from <a href="http://www.manchesterminerals.co.uk/cgi/sh000008.pl?REFPAGE=http%3a%2f%2fwww.manchesterminerals.co.uk%2facatalog%2fLapidary_accessories.html&amp;WD=lap&amp;PN=Lapidary_accessories.html%23a11_2d107#a11_2d107">Manchester Minerals</a>. These discs are for use with diamond paste. I have some 5 micron paste and some 1 micron paste that I bought from the company.
