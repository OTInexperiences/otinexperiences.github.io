What good is a 3D printer for Ornamental Turning I can imagine hearing that question coming from many members of the SOT and OTI around the world.  Well I ought to have some answer shouldn't I.  But I can't quite answer that yet. I will say that I have three aims
<ol>
<li>To have something small that I have on my desk and can use to amuse myself mainly in winter when I cannot get into the shed to do any plain or ornamental turning.</li>
<li>To be able to prototype ideas for bits and bobs to help with both plain and ornamental turning.</li>
<li>To quickly produce expendable items that can be used and thrown away when broken, again to help with either plain or ornamental turning.</li>
</ol>
Aim 1 is fulfilled as to whether aims 2 and 3 are realized only the future will show that.
