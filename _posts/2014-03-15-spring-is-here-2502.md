At last this week it has been warm enough to get back into the shed. I spent my time making a few items for my existing cnc rose engine.  Firstly I needed a bracket to hold the small proxxon spindle I bought last year. I intend to use this spindle for etching patterns into small items.  My cutting frame is capable of running at 7000 rpm but this is not really fast enough for an etching bit. Hence the proxxon which can run at up to 30,000 rpm.  I decided to make the bracket out of plastic using my 3D printer as the loading is light.

<p style="text-align: left;">The case of the proxxon spindle is also plastic so I designed my bracket to have an insert that is pushed against the spindle by a  threaded bolt passing through a nut that is held in an hexagonal rebate in the top of the bracket.</p>
<p style="text-align: left;"><a href="{{ '/assets/recovered/388c59ffae-bracket1.png' | relative_url }}"><img alt="bracket1" class="aligncenter size-medium wp-image-2492" height="158" src="{{ '/assets/recovered/8c8dcdd353-bracket1-300x158.png' | relative_url }}" width="300"/></a></p>
And here it is attached to the  Y axis of my tool post using a large washer to spread the load on the plastic, I ran a couple of tests and it works well.

I also needed a chuck to hold the items I will be etching and it is critical that the parts to be cut are perfectly perpendicular to the cutter. Otherwise the thickness of the line made by the cutter will vary or not exist,  so I spent a couple of days creating a <a href="{{ '/rose-engine-mark-2/accessories/leveling-plate/' | relative_url }}" title="Leveling Plate">leveling plate</a> and also a bracket to hold my dial gauge securely on the tool-post.

<a href="{{ '/assets/recovered/88a741ed9a-levelling-plate3.png' | relative_url }}"><img alt="levelling-plate3" class="aligncenter size-medium wp-image-2500" height="277" src="{{ '/assets/recovered/60eff39466-levelling-plate3-300x277.png' | relative_url }}" width="300"/></a>

Finally I bolted a small carving clamp bought at the last SOT meeting onto a magnetic block to form a holder for the head of my extractor hose (which is connected to a small cyclone type vacuum cleaner).

<a href="{{ '/assets/recovered/59aad35ba5-hose-holder2.png' | relative_url }}"><img alt="hose-holder2" class="aligncenter size-medium wp-image-2495" height="227" src="{{ '/assets/recovered/9dd50d2a82-hose-holder2-300x227.png' | relative_url }}" width="300"/></a>This allows me to clamp the block onto my tool post and position the hose directly under my cutting frame etc to remove the dust almost at source.
