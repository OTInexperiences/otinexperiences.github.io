Having cast my pewter medallions I have to find some way to hold them for machining. So this afternoon I decided to make some soft jaws out of Aluminum for my small chuck.  I can then machine a recess in them so that they will grip my medallion blanks. I think they turned out ok.

</figure>

This small chuck just fits in the jaws of the chuck on my metal lathe. So I can mount my small chuck in my metal lathe in order to skim the surface of the cast blank and make it flat and parallel.

I tried cutting a pattern again using a 2mm diameter end mill but not to pleased with the result. I will try cutting the pattern again tomorrow using my cutting frame to see what difference that makes.
