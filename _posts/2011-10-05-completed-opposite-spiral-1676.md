I decided to cut the opposite spiral to see the effect. My intention was (and still is) to make a matching pair of boxes one of whose lids had a clockwise spiral and the other an anticlockwise spiral.

As I cut the second spiral I thought about the first box and decided that the spalted sycamore base on the first box  just didn't seem appropriate, it did not look right. So back to looking for a suitable base for both boxes. I am considering using ripple sycamore for the box bases (as it is a light wood ). On each side of the base I thought of cutting a pattern using my geometric chuck simulator using a small etching cutter. My intention is then to fill this groove with a mixture of ebony dust (I collected whilst milling the tops) and either woodglue, superglue or resin. I hope the final effect will be similar to an ebony inlay. Anyway thats what I hope. So I am now considering  which patterns would look best on a 100mm x 100mm piece and also how to arrange the patterns. The observant amongst you will notice that the lids are different sizes. I will sand them down to the same size for the completed boxes.
