This pattern is 180mm diameter. I took a piece of mahogany (I think) and cut a recess in it 5mm deep on my wood lathe. Then filled it with clear-ish (I tried adding a small amount of mother of pearl coloring but not really enough) resin about 4mm deep. When set I cut the pattern 4mm deep so that the tool just cut into the wood. Using a small paintbrush I then used a dark mahogany spirit stain to highlight the pattern. When the stain was dry I turned about 1mm off the top (to remove any staining to the resin) and covered the whole with clear resin just enough to slightly cover the wooden edge and make a nice rounded edge to the piece.
