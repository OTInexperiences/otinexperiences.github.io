Yesterday when I made my mandrel to hold the gear blanks I held it in my metal lathe chuck to machine it. It seemed ok but was a little out of true. This became very noticeable when I put the chuck back on my wood lathe, there was a distinct wobble. You could not see it on the rose engine as that machine rotates very slowly.  Also I made it 10mm diameter which I found to be a bit large for the size of gear I am cutting.

So today I made another mandrel this time 8mm diameter.  To keep things concentric I held my wood lathe chuck in the chuck on my metal lathe and turned the mandrel in situ.
