I am interested in casting the wheels patterns in media other than wood, so here is the first attempt (it will get better).

I started by cutting a recess in a scrap piece of osb and filling with resin. I then cut a pattern in the  resin.

<a href="{{ '/assets/recovered/9afa1442e4-pattern-master.png' | relative_url }}"><img alt="pattern-master" class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-2247" height="225" src="{{ '/assets/recovered/9d36060ac5-pattern-master-300x225.png' | relative_url }}" width="300"/></a>

I poured some silicone rubber over this pattern in order to make a mold

<a href="{{ '/assets/recovered/7d3861814f-silicone-mould.png' | relative_url }}"><img alt="silicone-mould" class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-2245" height="225" src="{{ '/assets/recovered/d1730c5334-silicone-mould-300x225.png' | relative_url }}" width="300"/></a>

Today I melted some pewter and poured this into the cold mold to see what the result was.

<a href="{{ '/assets/recovered/df47b430e1-first-cast.png' | relative_url }}"><img alt="first-cast" class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-2251" height="225" src="{{ '/assets/recovered/0e72ff6cb2-first-cast-300x225.png' | relative_url }}" width="300"/></a>

Not very spectacular. There are too many air bubbles in the resultant casting. So I need to try again. I will re-melt the pewter and this time pre-heat the mold in the oven to get it to about 230 degrees before pouring the pewter. Hopefully this will stop any problems associated with expanding air bubbles.

I also hope to cast some slip clay and make some ceramic versions of the wheels patterns.
