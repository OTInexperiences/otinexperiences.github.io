I have completed turning the delrin bushes and printing out the plastic bearing blocks. Also the <a href="http://openbuildspartstore.com/v-slot/?sort=bestselling">V-Slot</a> bought from their agent <a href="http://www.robocutters.co.uk/categories.asp?cat=Vslot+and+Accessories">Robocutters.co.uk</a> here in the UK, has been cut to size and the carriage wheels assembled and fixed. The component now can move from side to side and forward and backwards.

<a href="{{ '/assets/recovered/75ead886cb-rose3-spindle1.png' | relative_url }}"><img alt="rose3-spindle1" class="aligncenter size-medium wp-image-2459" height="206" src="{{ '/assets/recovered/2e6518c149-rose3-spindle1-300x206.png' | relative_url }}" width="300"/></a>

I have left the back bearing top off to show the delrin bush bearing. Also I have to support the rails on a couple of books in order to allow them to run (because the nuts fastening the carriage wheels are a bit lower than the aluminium section. This does not matter because the whole thing will be attached to standard 20x40 profile frame (section on order).

Next part is to design and print the spindle motor frame , and the two rosette holders plus their drives etc. After several preliminary bearing prints I am beginning to run low on plastic filament.
