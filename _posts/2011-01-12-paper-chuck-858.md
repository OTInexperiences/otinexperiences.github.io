Just finished making my paper chuck so that I can test patterns before they are cut. It has a working diameter of approx 303mm (13 inches) .

<a href="http://otinexperiences.co.uk/blog/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/IMG_0034.jpg"><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-medium wp-image-855" height="300" src="/assets/placeholder-original-image.svg" title="IMG_0034" width="289"/></a>

To make it I bought a chuck back plate (from Axminster as I have their chuck) to fit M33 x3.5 . Then took a piece of 5mm Aluminium plate, bored that to 3mm depth and 40mm diameter to fit over the registration mark on the back plate and also drilled  4 holes equally spaced around a 800mm diameter and tapped for m6 thread.  I bolted the back plate and my aluminium plate together with three m8 cap head screws.  Then I screwed a 15 inches diameter piece of 5mm mdf onto the aluminium plate using  4  m6 pan headed bolts into the tapped holes. Finally I glued a circular piece of perspex 10mm thick onto the mdf. This perspex piece that I was given determined the size of the chuck.  When the perspex was firmly stuck I drilled 8 holes around the outside of the chuck to hold the rim piece (turned from a piece of mdf) which traps the paper.  I used some wing nuts to hold the rim in place (need to get a couple more).
