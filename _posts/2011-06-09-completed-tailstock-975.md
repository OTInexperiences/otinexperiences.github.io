I want to cut spiral patterns down the side of a piece. On the assumption that the other end of longer pieces would need supporting I have just completed building a tailstock. As this needs to move as the headstock moves I made it an overhead tailstock fitted to the headstock of my rose engine, see image below

<a href="http://otinexperiences.co.uk/blog/wp-content/uploads/2011/06/IMG_0232.jpg"><img alt="" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-976" height="344" src="/assets/placeholder-original-image.svg" title="IMG_0232" width="640"/></a>

The sizes are such that I can fit a piece of up to 300mm (12 inces) diameter  and 300mm long between the headstock and tailstock. More information about its construction is  <a href="{{ '/rose-engine-mark-2/accessories/tailstock/' | relative_url }}" title="Tailstock">here</a>
