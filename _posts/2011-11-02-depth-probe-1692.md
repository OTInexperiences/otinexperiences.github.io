I have spent the last couple of weeks building a renishaw type depth probe. I got the outline and basic idea from <a href="http://www.vinland.com/Touch-Probe.html">this site</a> on the web, although the size I finally ended up with was determined by the size of aluminum bar stock I had around.  Though it had to fit in the 43mm holder I created to first hold my proxxon mill, and then more recently my <a href="{{ '/blog/drilling-spindle/' | relative_url }}" title="Drilling Spindle">drilling spindle</a>. I used aluminium for the case and black delrin for the non conductive parts. I bought some 5mm ball bearings for the contacts, but had extreme difficulty in  soldering a wire onto them (my ineptness) in fact I failed.  So I machined a set of 6 brass pins each with a 5mm hemisperical end, which I made a push fit into the delrin base ring. One advantage of doing this was that I was able to drill a small 2mm hole in each pin to use as a soldering point. The contacts were some pieces of 4mm silver steel rod. Otherwise my design was exactly as on the web site mentioned above.
