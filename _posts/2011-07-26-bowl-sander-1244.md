A short while ago my power drill broke, recently I mostly used it with a sanding pad to sand the bowls I turn.  I havent replaced the drill yet so I am having to sand bowls by hand again. To remedy this I spent an afternoon in the workshop making one of those manual holders for a sanding pad, the ones that rotate when held against the work. It required just two small roller bearings and a bit of steel bar.
