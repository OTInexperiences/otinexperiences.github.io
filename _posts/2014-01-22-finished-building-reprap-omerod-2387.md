Just finished building and commissioning my Ormerod printer from a kit designed by <a href="http://www.reprappro.com/" title="reprappro">RepRapPro</a>.  The image below shows the first (and only ) thing I have printed so far. It is part of the commissioning process and is used to check that the axes of the printer are at 90 degrees to each other. If they are slightly out as mine were then this can be compensated for in the printing software.

<a href="{{ '/assets/recovered/98cb8008f7-3-IMG_0026.png' | relative_url }}"><img alt="3-IMG_0026" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-2390" height="939" src="{{ '/assets/recovered/98cb8008f7-3-IMG_0026.png' | relative_url }}" width="1227"/></a>

I haven't yet got round to tidying up the cabling by tying them together so it all looks a bit spaghetti like but not worried about that at the moment. Still on a learning curve about how to use the printer. I must say that the support given by RepRapPro is excellent also there is a very helpful <a href="http://forums.reprap.org/list.php?340">forum</a>. So this is my displacement activity whilst I cannot get into the shed over winter whilst it is too cold.
