I have recently produced a couple of items which have lids containing objects embedded in clear resin. The example here shows a slice of polished stone. I  cut through the underside of the lid so that the stone was visible there as well. The box is turned from sycamore and is about 8 inches in diameter and 6 inches high. Inside it contains two removable  trays.
