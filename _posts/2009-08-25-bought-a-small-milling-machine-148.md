Hooray,

I bought a small milling machine today so along with my metal lathe I feel I might become more of a model engineer than a wood turner. My intention is twofold. Firstly to use it to build a small tabletop cnc controlled rose engine so I can move indoors during January and February as I find that it is too cold to work in my shed during those months. This machine will use linear bearings and ballscrews for accuracy and I didnt think that I could build it without the accurate positioning a mill would give me. But secondly I can add steppers to the mill and build myself a proper cnc system.

So much fun and backache on thursday when its delivered.
