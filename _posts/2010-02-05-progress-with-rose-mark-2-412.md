I have made progress with the headstock. The ball screw and nut for the lateral movement are fitted and working. The runners for horizontal movement (squash) are fitted. Now I just need to 1) add the stepper for lateral movement, 2) machine the ball screw for squash movement. 3) Complete the toolpost.

You can see below the headstock with the Chuck from my wood lathe mounted. The flat aluminum plate will be the basis for a movable tool post. I hope to be able to generate Rose patterns on bowls / platters up to 18 inches diameter.
