After yesterdays problems I swapped over to my second rosette carrier and everything was smooth. So looked at the first rosette carrier and found that the grub screw holding the timing pulley on the rosette carrier was loose. This would account for the jerkiness as the grub screw is tightened onto a flat on the carrier axle. It must of been slipping from one end of the flat to the other. So I screwed it up and added some Locktight and everything is ok now. Must have forgotten to tighten the screw. A quick test showed that I can run at speeds of up to 25 rev per minute before losing contact with my one and only rosette. So hopefully if I run the main spindle at 1 rev per minute I can cut a 25 times repeated pattern of a rosette.
