Still needs a paint job and end of  the lead screw  fixing but stepper is connected and is working.  It is, as intended, a hefty lump of metal which should dampen down any vibrations.

Whilst making this I thought that it would be nice if I could move the cutting frame vertically as well as horizontally. That way I could cut small patterns in several places around the edge  a platter or simulate a straight line engine.

After thinking about how I could add a stepper onto the Myford vertical slide I realized that it was not necessary. I already had a slide that I made for  my original tool post which with a bit of re-jigging could be bolted onto the Myford slide whenever required.

You can see the stepper driven slide in the picture above. I still have to machine and mount a tool holder onto it and cut a relief slot in the back plate so that the acme nut housing does not foul onto the bolts fastening my stepper slide onto the Myford slide, but that is just a small job for sometime in the next couple of days.

Finally, I realized that I could also fasten my  stepper driven slide horizontally onto the  tool post base (replacing the Myford slide) and recover my X-Y table in a more suitable form for OT.
