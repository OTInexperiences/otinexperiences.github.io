I was trying too hard to get my bowl finished for tomorrow, but made a big mistake. The pattern was cut correctly (given yesterdays mistake)  through most of the wood but when I came to cast the pewter I found that the only ingot I had was insufficient!!!

The shame is that I will turn most of it away when I hollow the bowl. Must think of a better way of doing this.

The positive part of this is that I found the casting easy and clean. I melted the pewter in a small milk pan (bought specially for the job tonight from Sainsburys) on the hob in the kitchen, leveled the bowl (it is honestly) and poured. I noticed that pewter did not flow as freely as the resin I had used previously, which would have rushed around the pattern filling all the lowest levels. The pewter was more sluggish, probably because it was solidifying as it hit the cold wood. No real smoke just a bit from the wood so no complaints from my wife. I will definitely try this again. I may try to finish by pouring some more into my bowl although I understand that this probably won't work as casting is supposed to be completed in one go. So I may have to destroy the bowl to recover the pewter (its more expensive than the wood and my time costs nothing nowadays). Such is life.
