I connected a driver to my engine's motor for the first time today. The results were rather hit and miss.  The hit is that the stepper I bought is powerful enough to rotate the rosette against the rubber. The miss is that the  belt connecting the motor to the rosette axis has some slack and this is causing an uneven movement of the rosette. It is tending to snatch when rotating from the highest amplitude to the lowest. This is the effect of the spring  drives the rosette around  until the slackness in the belt is taken up.

The amplitude change of my rosette is 20mm and this may be unrealistically large for such a small machine as mine. I will have to try to tighten the belt so as to minimise this movement but I am not sure if I will ever remove it totally.  So I may have to re-design the drive.
