I have most recently been busy creating a tool post which is driven by a stepper motor. The movement is again via linear bearings running on 10mm hardened steel shafts and driven by a 12mm acme screw thread with 3mm pitch running through a brass nut. I bought all these components and constructed a base out of aluminum.
<p style="text-align: center;"></p>

The tool post has a range of 150mm (6 inches) and may be clamped anywhere along the movable bed. If I were to make this part again I would use larger diameter rails and make the whole thing a bit beefier.

The cutting frame is universal in that the cutter may be rotated to cut both horizontally and vertically and all angles in-between.

<a href="http://otinexperiences.co.uk/blog/wp-content/uploads/2010/10/img_0045-cf-4.jpg"><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-medium wp-image-718" height="251" src="/assets/placeholder-original-image.svg" title="img_0045 (cf-4)" width="300"/></a>

The head and shaft are separate so that other heads may be used. I have a smaller head that holds a small hss bar.
