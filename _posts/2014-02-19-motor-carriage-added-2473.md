Just added the stepper carriage to spindle. I found I did not have the required bolts to fix it correctly so some ordered. So apologies for the scrappy way the motor is bolted onto the spindle. This is also just an old spare stepper I had around, it is not the one that will be attached to the final thing. That too is on order. I have used standard 40x20 aluminium profile to act as a base and connect the two base VSlot sections.  I know that the 25mm T5 belt looks a little like overkill, but I think it is ok.

I took the machine as displayed here to the SOT quarterly meeting this last Saturday, there was some interest I am happy to say. I particularly wanted to show that 3D printers could be useful tools for helping design new machines. By the way the white plastic is because I ran out of black. Also for information I made two previous versions of the motor holder before I ended with this final (so far) design.
