After casting and machining the pewter medallions I cut a couple of patterns one on each side. The first side was cut to a depth of 0.1mm with an engraving bit held in my drilling spindle.

The reverse side of the medallion was cut with a 120 degree v tool running vertically in my cutting frame using exactly the same petal pattern as above but with only the two outer rows of petals.
