I have just finished cutting a spiral on the underside of an Ash platter which is 12 inches in diameter.  I plain turned the platter then ran my depth probe across its underside to create a profile. Then I wrote a simple g code program to cut a simple spiral whilst following the contour of the underside of the platter.

Unfortunately it was not too successful, firstly the profile I ended up with was not smooth and this showed up as ridges in the finished surface. I think that when taking profile readings my probe sank into the softer parts of the grain. The curve looked ok on the Emc pre-plot but the eye is much more sensitive and picks up the surface ripples.

Secondly the grain on the ash tended to  break out slightly near the edge when the curve of the platter became more pronounced.  I was using a very slow feed rate and had sharpened the cutter but obviously need to practice getting the cutter much sharper than I can manage at present.

Finally, I should not have finished the underside  with a lacquer finish, a nice mat oiled finish would have been better.  I left the top of the platter plain turned and was happy with the smooth curve I achieved and finished this with a food safe oil.
