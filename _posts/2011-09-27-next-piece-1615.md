Just completed my first version of the box that Bill Ooms described in the Fall 2010 OTI newsletter. The differences are that mine is made from a piece of walnut and is a 100 x 100 x 100 mm (4 x 4 x 4) cube. Also where Bill used a frequency 8 sine rosette with a peek to peak amplitude of 0.05 inches and each cut moves over by 0.1 inches. I used a frequency 7 sine with amplitude 4mm (giving a positive to negative peak amplitude of 8mm) and moved the pattern over by also 4mm for each cut. I used the same 90 degree phase shift as Bill. Bill made his cuts to a depth of 0.05 inches whilst I set the zero position to be on the cube surface left the final cutter depth at zero so as to make the final maximum cut depth to be that of the rose amplitude which was 4mm, did this in two 2mm stages by setting the cutter depth on the first pass to be +2mm so that it lay nominally above the surface of the cube. Finally, after turning out the centre of the box I coated it with a layer of gold antiquing cream to give a gold finish to the inside. And blasphemously because the wood was walnut (and not good for ornamental turning) I buffed up the resultant pattern in three stages in the same manner as I treat my bowls instead of leaving just the tooled finish.
