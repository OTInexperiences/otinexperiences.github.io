For various reasons, I have recently been concentrating on the quick-view display rather than on the full 3D display. So currently the 3D display is not functioning correctly. I am thinking of removing the 3D display altogether. Initially I started with the 3D display because at that time Emc2 did not correctly show paths that involved rotations. This has now been corrected in Emc2 and so my 3D display is superfluous.  Anyway my 3D display needs a major rewrite and I would much rather concentrate on other things at this time.
