For some time I have wanted to cut the patterns I have in my blog banner. Having obtained a largeish piece of Delrin I decided to make a start. The image below is 16 inches (400mm ) diameter and is the largest piece I have cut so far.

<a href="{{ '/assets/recovered/34145f09f8-IMG_0027.png' | relative_url }}"><img alt="IMG_0027" class="aligncenter size-medium wp-image-2273" height="225" src="{{ '/assets/recovered/77c3c0b0fa-IMG_0027-300x225.png' | relative_url }}" width="300"/></a>

It was cut with a 1mm diameter ball ended carbide cutter to a depth of 0.8mm. I used my makeshift leveling chuck to  get the piece to run true to within 0.4mm. Even so the plate was not flat and my initial cut of 0.5mm left some parts untouched. I did not want to use a cutter to plane the face true as I wanted to keep the shiny finish given by its production. Even so there are some scratches that I will try to polish out using Tcut or something similar.
