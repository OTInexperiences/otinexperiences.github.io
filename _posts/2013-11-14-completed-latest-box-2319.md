Just finished this box with a spiral pattern cut on top its top and which is repeated inside the lid and under the base. It is 200mm diameter and 80mm deep, the finial is also 80mm high. The finial is black wood but I am not sure what the box is made from.  I bought a couple of planks from a SOT auction labelled mahogany and this is one of them although there was some doubt at last nights wood turning meeting as to it being mahogany.

I think the spiral shape is fairly elegant but it was the result of a fortuitous mistake. There are 24 flutes on the spiral giving a 15 degree separation between each flute. The g code to cut the spiral is a simple linear move from a starting point 25mm from the centre and ending 100mm from the centre, whilst rotating the work through a further two angle increments (i.e 30 degrees) . I decided that this starting point was too far from the centre leaving a fairly large flat area under the finial. So I then made another pass this time starting from 10mm from the centre again going to 100mm whilst rotating the same amount.  As you can see (and probably already realize)  this results in a different spiral. Fortunately for me it gave a pleasing effect as the flutes widened out and looked much better than the original. The finial is an egg shape with again 24 small cuts around its circumference. The box was finished by buffing, applying a hard oil finish and when dry carnuba wax was applied using a buffing wheel.
