Sorry, I have not posted anything in the last few months but that does not mean I have not been doing things.

I made a coffee table from a piece of burr elm.

The legs were made from long segments   glued together  capped at each end with a solid piece and  then turned cylindrical. They are screwed into the table base with 12mm allthread rod  studs into aluminium fittings glued with epoxy into rebates made in the top of each leg  and  into fittings also glued in the underside of the table.
