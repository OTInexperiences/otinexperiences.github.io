My name is Alan and I am retired living in the UK. My main hobbies are wood turning, ornamental turning, increasingly model engineering and lastly computer programming which was part of my job and still is a major interest. This blog is to explain my experiences in the construction and use of  a simple and hopefully cheap cnc system based on stepper motors to be used for ornamental turning. I am in the process of building a fairly crude (in accuracy terms) system which bolts onto my wood turning lathe. As a wood turner I will be satisfied with achieving a repeatable accuracy of  (Engineers throw up your hands in despair now!!) around 0.1 mm. My intention is to turn items and then decorate their tops, sides etc with patterns cut into the material.

Along with the construction of the hardware, which is mostly finished, I am also writing some software, far from being finished,  that I pretentiously call the Ornamental Turning Workbench or (OTWB). I will use this software to generate something  called <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gcode" target="_blank">gcode</a>, which for those of you who know nothing about cnc, is the code used to control cnc machines.  I have a Linux system on a laptop running a free cnc controller called <a href="http://www.linuxcnc.org/" target="_blank">Emc</a>, an excellent piece of software, which controls my stepper motors through the parallel port. I do intend to make my software freely available from this site when I have an alpha version which I hope will be soon.
