Whilst making the cube box I was looking for some way to hold the piece in my axminster chuck whilst I decorated each face.  I bought some small expandable faceplate jaws 150mm diameter a while ago and had been using this with some wooden attachments screwed to the jaws and then turned with a recess to hold the work. However one of the attached wooden jaws broke so I looked for an alternative. It was then that I noticed that the screw holes in the faceplate jaws could be tapped out to take a 6mm thread. This enabled me to screw the rubber buttons from my large cole jaws onto the faceplate jaws giving me a nice firm gripping surface.
