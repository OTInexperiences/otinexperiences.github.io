Finally got round to cutting a pattern on the oak platter shown mounted on the chuck in my earlier pictures. I want to cut through most of the bowl just leaving about 2mm at the bottom of the cut. I intend to then fill cuts with molten pewter and skim both sides of the platter when solidified so that the pattern shows on both sides. This is my first attempt at this, hope it looks alright in the end and that the pewter stays where it should. Will try to cut small tabs in the wall of the cut to help hold solidified pewter. Having some problems though with cutting the pattern. My Proxxon spindle is not meant to be run for extended periods and is getting quite hot. I am cutting in 1.5mm depth increments per pass and am stopping after every two passes to let the spindle cool. I had to change my cutter after the first pattern cut and unfortunately I did not get the new cutter in exactly the right position so I need a more accurate method of changing cutters, will have to think about that.
