Just completed my drilling spindle to replace my proxxon mill head. I bought a ER16 chuck with 12mm parallel spindle (plus collets) as the basis. Placed this in an aluminium case supported by two 12mm roller bearings and then turned a pulley and fixed this to the spindle using grub screws.  I bored the pulley out to 16mm diameter because my larger boring bar is too large to handle  a 12mm hole. I then turned two small brass packing sleeves 16mm outside diameter and  bored these to 12mm using my small boring bar. One fits either end of the pulley and holds everything nice and concentric.
