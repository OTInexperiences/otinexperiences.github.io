Progressing towards a complete system, I have just build a polishing wheel to hone the cutters that I make (made one so far). The table was made from scrap bits and pieces I had around the workshop, I use my Tormek grindstone to drive the wheel through some polychord belting.

<figure class="wp-recovered-caption">
<a href="http://otinexperiences.co.uk/blog/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/IMG_0004.jpg"><img alt="" class="size-large wp-image-890" height="373" src="/assets/placeholder-original-image.svg" title="IMG_0004" width="640"/></a>
<figcaption>Wheel for polishing my cutters</figcaption>
</figure>

The wheel is a 6 inch diameter aluminium round topped off with a perspex top. It sits on a thrust bearing and the bottom of the spindle is held in a ball race.

<figure class="wp-recovered-caption">
<a href="http://otinexperiences.co.uk/blog/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/IMG_0006.jpg"><img alt="" class="size-large wp-image-891" height="395" src="/assets/placeholder-original-image.svg" title="IMG_0006" width="384"/></a>
<figcaption>Underside view of polishing wheel</figcaption>
</figure>

There is a double  pulley on the underside in order to provide a drive to sweep the cutter back and forth across the wheel. This is not built yet

I have to cover the perspex plate with something to hold the polishing medium. I am thinking about having replaceable covers holding different polishing media. Such as the honing paste used with the Tormek or some diamond paste as an alternative.
