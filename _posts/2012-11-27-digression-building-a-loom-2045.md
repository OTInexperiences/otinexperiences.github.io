Weaving has a strong family connection, as both my parents were associated with the weaving industry. My mother worked as a weaver in the mills in Blackburn and my father worked as a Tackler who is basically someone who looked after the looms and kept them in working order.

When one of my daughters expressed an interest in hand weaving so I thought it would be a good idea to have a go at making a loom. After looking around the Internet I came across David Holly's <a href="http://www.pvcloom.com/?page_id=16" title="pvc loom">site  </a>and bought his book which I think is an excellent description of how to build a loom cheaply, for someone who hasn't any practical engineering or woodworking experience nor tools such as a lathe and a mill etc. Dave is an extremely helpful chap and has readily replied to the many questions I have asked so far.

For me this is almost full circle as 48 years ago I left school  in Blackburn and started an apprenticeship with a company called <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Loom" target="_blank" title="British Northrop Ltd">British Northrop Ltd</a> supplier of looms to the mills of Lancashire and the rest of the world. I left after 3 years as they sponsored me to go to University and then unfortunately went bust a couple of years later. So I never worked for them as an engineer.
