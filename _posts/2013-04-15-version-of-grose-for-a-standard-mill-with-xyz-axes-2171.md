I have edited the original Grose program to make it easier to use with a standard milling machine setup. That is No rotary table, depth movement in the Z direction and workpiece movement in the X and Y directions.  The depth direction is at 90 degrees to the mill table. Also there is no pumping motion in this setup .

I have included the ability to cut a pattern along a profile with some limitations.

See the <a href="{{ '/grose-for-use-on-a-standard-mill/' | relative_url }}" title="Grose for use on a standard mill">Information page</a>.
