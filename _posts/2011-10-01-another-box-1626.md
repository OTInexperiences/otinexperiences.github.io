The walnut box I cut did not really do justice to the pattern that Bill Ooms described in the OTI newsletter because the wood itself is really not good for ornamental turning and in order to compensate for this I made the pattern a little larger than I should have and it ruined the effect. So I decided to cut it again but on a piece of ebony which would display to a much better effect. However as I only had enough ebony for a lid I made a box base from spalted sycamore.

The box is 100mm x 100mm x 100mm and like the previous box the pattern was a 7 lobed sine but this time 2mm amplitude and the cutter was moved 3mm across after each cut.  These values are still much greater than those used by Bill but I think the effect is quite reasonable on a lid 100mm square.
