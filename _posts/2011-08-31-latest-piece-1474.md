Just completed another box. The diameter is 125mm and it is 100mm high.  The lid is cut from a piece of tulipwood that I was given and the body is a piece of lime.

<img alt="img_0459" class="ngg-singlepic ngg-center" height="368" src="/assets/placeholder-original-image.svg" width="448"/>

I cut a wave into the top using my cutting frame which took quite a long while. Then I cut another wave into the side using my drilling spindle with a small etching cutter.
