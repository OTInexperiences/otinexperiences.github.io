### About This Website

This website is to be a recreation of my dad's original blog, [www.otinexperiences.co.uk/blog](https://otinexperiences.co.uk/blog).

It is currently being rebuilt using web scrapers, old photographs, and a WordPress backup I've luckily managed to recover. I'm doing this in order to preserve his work, memories, and the little piece of the internet he created.

My aim is to keep the spirit of the original blog alive while making it accessible for years to come.

You can find my dad's original code on [GitHub](https://github.com/alanbattersby).

Alongside restoring the original content, I also hope to add some posts of my own, continuing the blog in my own way while staying true to its roots. Apple trees and the like!

I've already rewritten and extended some of his original software in Python to bring it up to date, and I hope to make this available here in early 2027, alongside the relaunch of his blog.

To distinguish between the original blog and my own contributions, all restored posts will retain my dad's name and their original publication dates wherever possible. Any new posts, software updates, or additions will be clearly identified as being written or created by me.

I do hope you'll stick around, and that one day I might contribute to the OT community just as my father did.
