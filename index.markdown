This website is an ongoing recreation of my dad's original blog,  [www.otinexperiences.co.uk/blog]({{ '/blog/' | relative_url }}).

It's currently being rebuilt using web scrapers, old photographs and a WordPress backup I've luckily managed to recover. I'm doing this to preserve his work, his memories and the little piece of the internet he created.

My ultimate aim is to keep the spirit of the original blog alive while making it accessible for years to come. Alongside restoring the original content, I may one day add some posts of my own, hopefully continuing the blog in my own way while staying true to its roots.

Apples, falling trees and the like :)

If I do, then to distinguish between the original blog and my own contributions, all restored posts and pages will retain my dad's name and their original publication dates wherever possible. Any new posts, software updates or additions will be clearly identified as being written or created by me.

You can find my dad's original code on his [GitHub](https://github.com/alanbattersby).
